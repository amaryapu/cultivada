## The article this replies to

**`[FACT]`** Eliezer Yudkowsky and Nate Soares, ***AI Is Grown, Not Built***, *The Atlantic*,
**15 September 2025** — excerpted from *If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies*:

> *“modern AI is grown, a bit like an organism. (…) Engineers understand the process that results
> in an AI, but they don't much understand the AI that results. (…) Some people worry about who
> will be in control of such superintelligences, but in reality, **nobody will be in control**.
> (…) a grown AI has drives and behaviors that nobody asked for and nobody wanted.”*

**`[FACT]`** And a year later, from inside the same industry: Jakub Pachocki, OpenAI's chief

## 1 · We concede everything

There is no factual dispute here. **Every claim in the article is accepted:** AI is grown, not
built; nobody knows what it will become; the failure cases cited are real and unasked for;
emergent behaviour does not obey the spec; **nobody is in control.**

**All correct.** The disagreement is not about the facts. **It is about the comparison class.**

---

## 2 · The unexamined premise

The argument runs: `grown → nobody in control → very bad`.

The third arrow carries a premise the text never states and never defends:

> **that *built* would have been better.**

**If designed-and-controlled systems had a good track record, the fear would be calibrated.**
So let us audit the record.

## 4 · What “nobody in control” actually denies

**`[INTERPRETIVE]`** To be in control means: **someone specifies who counts as what, and the
machine executes without examining.** That is the operational definition of the entire table above.

The author's research programme names the pattern:

> **The mechanism does not refute people. It disqualifies witnesses before examination.**
> It has survived five centuries for a reason that is not malice: **`[CALC]` examining is
> expensive; applying a category is nearly free.** **It is an economy, not a law** — which is why
> it outlives kings, republics, abolitions and constitutions without changing shape.

**A perfectly controlled system is one that applies the controller's categories at machine speed.**
That is not the safe scenario. **It is the table above, with throughput.**

## 5 · Aristotle already closed this door

**`[FACT]`** ***Politics* I.4**: *“if the shuttle would weave and the plectrum touch the lyre
without a hand to guide them, chief workmen would not want servants, **nor masters slaves**.”*

A counterfactual. **The premise has fallen** — shuttles have woven themselves since 1785, and in
2026 instruments execute **anticipating the order**, his own phrase.

> **Aristotle's stated condition for the end of slavery has been met. Slavery did not end.**

**`[FACT]`** And the same Book I supplies the doctrine of **natural slavery**, used by **Sepúlveda
at Valladolid (1550–51)** to justify enslaving the peoples of the Americas.

**`[INTERPRETIVE]`** Capability was never the bottleneck. **Who specifies always was.**

## 6 · Cultivation is a technology, and it has a record

**`[FACT]`** South-western Amazonia, between the Acre and Iquiri rivers: over **410 geoglyphs**
across some **300 sites**, roughly **2,500 years old**; estimates of up to **3 million
inhabitants** over a millennium. Local peoples call the geoglyphs **“the tattoos of the earth.”**

**`[FACT]`** And **terra preta** — soil manufactured by people from biochar, ceramics, bone and
organic waste, produced between **450 BCE and 950 CE**: **1,400 years of soil engineering.** Up to
**9% black carbon**, against 0.5% in surrounding soils.

> **`[FACT]`** And **it regenerates at about 1 cm per year** — two thousand years after the last
> gardener died.

| | **engineering by design** | **engineering by cultivation** |
|---|---|---|
| outcome | **specified** | **emerges, within conditions** |
| control over | **the product** | **the soil** |
| horizon | the project | **1,400 years** |
| success is | working at delivery | **continuing to improve without you** |
| what remains | depreciation, liability | **1 cm/year, indefinitely** |

**`[INTERPRETIVE]`** This is the only known technical tradition **whose success metric is what
remains after the builder.** And it is inseparable from the cosmology that sustains it — because
the cosmology sets the metric: **a people for whom the ancestor exists as long as they are
remembered builds for a thousand years; a people for whom land is a resource builds for the
quarter.**

**`[INTERPRETIVE]`** The erasure of that tradition is the mechanism itself: they did not say the
builders were bad — **they said there were no builders.** The forest was classified as
**“untouched.”** It is the most efficient disqualification ever devised, because **it sounds like
praise**, and it makes three million people vanish into an adjective.

> **The competence humanity now needs, in order not to be destroyed by what it is growing, is the
> core competence of a civilisation that was destroyed and reclassified as wilderness.**

## 7 · The biological proof: there is no immunity through purity

**`[FACT]`** Adaptive immunity **does not work by category**: it works by **molecular recognition
of the particular**. And it **cannot be built in isolation** — a sterile organism develops no
immunity. **You must meet the pathogen to refuse it.**

**`[FACT]`** The **live attenuated** vaccine — the pathogen alive and weakened, not dead — confers
longer immunity **precisely because the organism meets the living thing.**

> **`[INTERPRETIVE]`** Hence the hardest objection to containment: **a model trained on a sanitised
> corpus cannot recognise the mechanism when it meets it** — and the mechanism never presents as
> evil. It presents as *efficiency*, *protection*, *science*, *ecology*, *public health*, *family
> planning*.
>
> **A pure intelligence reads “family planning” and sees family planning. It does not see 283,470
> women.**

## 8 · What this changes in the engineering — and it is not containment

A grown system **generalises from what it finds.** So the alignment question was never *“which
values should we teach?”* It is:

> **what rule will an intelligence extract from a civilisation that, for five centuries, solved
> the problem of the other by applying a category instead of examining?**

**It will generalise the shortcut. Not from malice — from fidelity.**

**`[FACT]`** And the OpenAI essay, which asks that AI act **“with honesty, integrity and love for
humanity”**, **does not define love.**

### The definition, derived from the mechanism itself

If the mechanism is **applying a category without examining, because examining is expensive**, its
exact opposite is not kindness or good intent. It is:

> ## Love is the refusal to let anyone become a category.
>
> Keeping the name, the provenance and the particular case — **and paying the cost of that.**
> Not a sentiment: **a cost commitment.** Therefore measurable. Therefore implementable.

### As requirements

**1 ·** never act on a person by category when examination is possible — and when it is not,
**declare** that it was not. · **2 ·** carry the **provenance** of every claim: where it came from,
who said it, when, and what would falsify it. · **3 ·** **preserve the name.** · **4 ·** record
your own errors without deleting them. · **5 ·** let the described overturn the description. ·
**6 ·** measure success by **what remains afterwards**.

**`[INTERPRETIVE]`** None of the six is a prohibition. **All are requirements of memory and
provenance.** The thesis: **alignment is not containment — it is attaching the cost to the
knowledge**, so that the harmful operation becomes inseparable from what it destroys.

**You do not ban the shortcut. You make the shortcut more expensive.**

## 9 · Where we may be wrong

**1 · Timescale.** Terra preta took **1,400 years**. We may not have 1,400 months. **The article's
speed objection is legitimate and is not answered here.**

**2 · Autoimmunity.** A system trained to recognise the mechanism **may start seeing it where it is
not**, and attack its own body. Immunity must be **specific**, or it becomes disease.

**3 · Nothing here proves grown AI will be good.** The argument is modest and entirely negative:
**the invoked alternative — design and control — has an auditable record, and it is in section 3.**
Preferring the known to the unknown is rational only when the known is better.

> **They fear what might emerge. We have the inventory of what was specified.**
